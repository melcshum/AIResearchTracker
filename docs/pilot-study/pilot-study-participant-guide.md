# Pilot Study: AI Wiki Companion

**Study Period:** 2 weeks  
**Target Participants:** 5-8 machine learning learners  
**Time Commitment:** 30-45 minutes per session  

---

## Study Overview

This pilot study evaluates the **AI Wiki Companion**, a tool designed to support knowledge construction through a 5-stage cycle:

1. **Construct** - Write your own explanation
2. **Reflect** - Answer metacognitive questions
3. **Scaffold** - Receive AI feedback on gaps
4. **Consolidate** - Apply knowledge to new scenarios
5. **Revisit** - Connect to related concepts

The tool implements the **Learner-in-the-Loop Knowledge Construction Framework**, which prioritizes epistemic agency—ensuring learners remain active constructors of knowledge rather than passive recipients of AI-generated content.

---

## Participant Instructions

### Session 1: Initial Knowledge Construction (30-45 minutes)

#### Step 1: Access the AI Wiki (2 minutes)
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to: `http://localhost:8000/ai-wiki.html`
3. You should see the AI Wiki interface with a sidebar on the right

#### Step 2: Select a Concept (2 minutes)
1. Choose one of the following ML concepts to explore:
   - **Overfitting** (recommended for beginners)
   - **Regularisation**
   - **Cross-validation**
2. Click on the concept name in the main content area

#### Step 3: Construct - Write Your Explanation (10 minutes)
1. In the AI Companion sidebar, click **"Construct"**
2. Write your explanation of the concept in your own words
   - What is it?
   - Why does it matter?
   - Can you give an example?
3. Click **"Save & Reflect"**
4. You should see: ✅ "Entry saved to your knowledge base"

#### Step 4: Reflect - Answer Metacognitive Questions (5 minutes)
1. The system will automatically generate reflection questions
2. Answer each question thoughtfully:
   - "What part of your explanation are you most confident about?"
   - "What part are you least confident about?"
   - "What questions remain unanswered?"
3. Click **"Continue to Scaffold"**

#### Step 5: Scaffold - Receive AI Feedback (5 minutes)
1. Click **"Detect Knowledge Gaps"**
2. The system will identify:
   - Missing related concepts
   - Suggestions for deeper exploration
3. Review the feedback and note any insights

#### Step 6: Consolidate - Apply Knowledge (10 minutes)
1. Click **"Consolidate"**
2. You'll be given a scenario to apply your knowledge:
   - Example: "You're training a model and notice high training accuracy but low validation accuracy. What's happening and what would you do?"
3. Write your response
4. Click **"Get Feedback"**
5. Review the consolidation task

#### Step 7: Revisit - Connect Concepts (5 minutes)
1. Click **"Revisit"**
2. The system will show related concepts (if you've explored them)
3. Note any connections between concepts

#### Step 8: Complete Feedback Form (5 minutes)
1. Fill out the post-session feedback form (link provided separately)
2. Answer all questions honestly—there are no right or wrong answers

---

### Session 2: Deepening Understanding (30-45 minutes)

**Repeat Session 1 with a different concept** or **revisit the same concept** to deepen your understanding.

**Goal:** Complete at least one full 5-stage cycle and note any changes in your understanding.

## What We're Measuring

### Quantitative Metrics
- **Interaction patterns:** Which modes do you use most?
- **Time spent:** How long in each stage?
- **Revision frequency:** How often do you update your explanations?

### Qualitative Feedback
- **Usability:** How easy is the tool to use?
- **Learning value:** Does it help you understand concepts better?
- **Epistemic agency:** Do you feel in control of your learning?
- **Suggestions:** What would you improve?

## Technical Notes

- **Data Storage:** All your entries are saved locally in your user profile
- **Privacy:** No data is sent to external servers
- **AI Mode:** Currently using mock responses (template-based scaffolding)
- **Browser:** Works best in Chrome, Firefox, or Safari

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Sidebar not appearing | Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R) |
| "Save failed" error | Check that the API server is running (contact researcher) |
| No reflection prompts | Try a different concept or refresh |
| Page not loading | Ensure you're using `http://localhost:8000/ai-wiki.html` |
