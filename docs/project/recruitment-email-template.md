## Tips for Successful Recruitment

### Do:
- ✅ Personalize emails (use their name, mention specific course/connection)
- ✅ Keep emails concise (under 200 words for initial contact)
- ✅ Be flexible with scheduling (offer multiple time slots)
- ✅ Follow up once (but don't spam)
- ✅ Offer incentives if budget allows
- ✅ Start early (recruitment takes longer than expected)

### Don't:
- ❌ Send generic mass emails (low response rate)
- ❌ Wait until the last minute to recruit
- ❌ Pressure people to participate
- ❌ Forget to track responses
- ❌ Skip the reminder emails (no-shows are common)
- ❌ Over-promise (be honest about time commitment)

---

## Expected Response Rates

- **Cold email (no prior relationship):** 10-20% response rate
- **Warm email (classmate/colleague):** 30-50% response rate
- **In-person recruitment:** 40-60% sign-up rate
- **Follow-up email:** Additional 10-15% response rate

**Target:** Send 20-30 emails → Get 6-10 responses → Schedule 5-8 sessions

---

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Low response rate**
- Solution: Personalize emails more, offer stronger incentive, recruit in-person

**Problem: High no-show rate**
- Solution: Send stronger reminders, offer rescheduling option, over-recruit by 20%

**Problem: Not enough participants**
- Solution: Extend recruitment period, expand to more courses/groups, reduce session length

**Problem: Scheduling conflicts**
- Solution: Offer more time slots (evenings/weekends), consider online sessions via Zoom
