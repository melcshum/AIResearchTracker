# Continuity equation on metric spaces via measure-valued derivations and BV-Wasserstein curves

**arXiv ID:** 2608.28586v1
**Authors:** Ehsan Abedi, Zhenhao Li, Timo Schultz
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28586v1
**Topics:** ai-agents

## Abstract

We introduce a notion of continuity equation on metric spaces that is capable of describing curves of probability measures which are absolutely continuous, and more generally of bounded variation (BV), with respect to the 1-Wasserstein distance. This continuity equation is based on a notion of measure-valued derivations, whose basic theory is also developed in this paper. On $\mathbb{R}^n$, our formulation is consistent with the continuity equation with singular flux introduced by Almi--Rossi--Savaré (arXiv:2506.15333), including the corresponding notion of minimal solutions. In this work, we characterize BV-curves in the space of probability measures equipped with the (extended) 1-Wasserstein distance as those curves satisfying the continuity equation with a measure-valued derivation of finite mass. To this aim, we extend our previous work (Calc.Var.(2024)63:16) on probabilistic representations on BV-curves and construct from them measure-valued derivations (resp. flux measures) on geodesic metric spaces (resp. on $\mathbb{R}^n$).
